THE COUNCIL

Ideas create dialogue. Dialogue creates change.

The Chanakya Council is an emerging platform dedicated to Model United Nations, debate, diplomacy, research and intellectual discourse.

Inspired by the strategic thought and statesmanship associated with Chanakya, the Council seeks to create spaces where young minds can question, negotiate, research and lead.

Our conferences aim to move beyond speeches — encouraging substantive debate, strategic diplomacy and meaningful resolutions.
